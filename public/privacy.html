Official Application Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for LIFTED

Effective Date: November 1, 2026
Application: LIFTED (Android Package: com.lifted.app)
Project Client: Cherie Magsanoc (Lifted)
Infrastructure Host: Every Nation GG

1. Introduction & Core Philosophy

LIFTED is a faith-based mental wellness companion designed for individuals and parents navigating emotional healing. We adhere to a strict privacy-by-design and local-first architecture. You do not need to provide personal identification or create an online account to use LIFTED.

2. Data Handling & Storage Architecture

A. Local Hardware Enclave Encryption:
Your personal reflections, prayers, and mood tracking entries are stored exclusively on your device. Field contents are encrypted using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. On native Android devices, data is stored in local app storage.

B. Sanctuary Privacy Lock:
The application provides an optional Sanctuary Lock that utilizes your device's native biometric prompt (Fingerprint / Face Unlock) or system device credential (PIN / Pattern) to gate access to the user interface. Biometric templates remain securely within your device's hardware security module and are never accessible to LIFTED.

C. Zero Cloud Synchronization in Current Release (v1.0.0):
Cloud synchronization is inactive by default in version 1.0.0. Your journals, prayers, and emotional logs remain 100% on your physical handset and are never transmitted to external cloud servers.

3. Optional Online Features & Authentication

If you choose to sign in to optional cloud services using email Magic Link / One-Time Password (OTP):

  • Your email address is transmitted securely to our backend infrastructure hosted on Supabase (managed by Every Nation GG) solely for authentication and session issuance.
  • Your personal reflections and prayer history remain on-device and are not automatically uploaded upon sign-in.

4. Crisis Support & Hotline Resources

The application includes country-specific crisis helpline directories (e.g., Philippines 911 / NCMH 1553, US 988 Lifeline). All crisis directory data is bundled offline within the application. Dialing a helpline initiates a standard telephone call through your device's native phone dialer; LIFTED records zero telemetry regarding emergency helpline usage.

5. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertising

LIFTED does not contain third-party advertising networks, commercial tracking SDKs, or analytics trackers. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information.

6. Account & Data Deletion Rights

Under Google Play Data Safety standards, you maintain the complete right to delete your data:

  • Local Data Reset: You can purge all on-device reflections, prayers, and cached data at any time via Journey → Preferences → Local Device Data Reset.
  • Online Account Deletion: If you registered an email account, you can submit a deletion request via our Account & Data Deletion Request Page.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

Any updates to this privacy policy will be posted to this page and announced in application release notes.
For questions regarding this policy or data safety inquiries, contact the application administrators via the Every Nation GG infrastructure team or through the account deletion request form.
