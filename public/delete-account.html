1. Data Architecture: Offline Guest vs. Authenticated Account

LIFTED operates on an offline-first, privacy-by-design model:

  • Guest Users (Default): All reflection journals, prayers, mood records, and breath pacer usage are stored exclusively on your local device within an AES-256-GCM encrypted vault. No records or telemetry are transmitted to our servers.
  • Authenticated Users: If you optional sign in via email Magic Link / OTP, your authentication identity (email address, user ID, session tokens) is stored securely by Supabase on behalf of Every Nation GG.

2. How to Delete Your Data

Option A: Delete On-Device Local Data (Immediate)

Open the LIFTED mobile app → Journey → Preferences → scroll to Local Device Data Reset → tap Reset Local Data. This permanently purges your encrypted database, mood history, and settings from the local hardware enclave.

Option B: Request Deletion of Online Cloud Account & Server Records

If you registered an email account and wish to permanently delete your server-side account, authentication credentials, and associated cloud records, submit your request below:

Your deletion request has been submitted. The infrastructure administrator (Every Nation GG) will process your account deletion and confirm via email.

3. Retention and Processing Commitment

Upon verified receipt of an account deletion request:

  • Your authentication record in Supabase Auth (auth.users) is permanently deleted.
  • Any role assignments (public.user_roles) and cloud metadata are permanently purged.
  • Local device records are unaffected until purged on-device via Option A.
