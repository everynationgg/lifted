# LIFTED — CLIENT DEMONSTRATION WALKTHROUGH
## 5–10 Minute Interactive Preview Script for Cherie Magsanoc (Client)

**Audience**: Cherie Magsanoc (Project Lead / Commissioning Stakeholder)  
**Presenter Role**: Senior Product Specialist & Engineering Lead  
**Tone**: Warm, compassionate, nontechnical, faith-anchored, and trauma-informed  
**Target Duration**: 8 to 10 Minutes  
**Demonstration Build**: `LIFTED-client-preview.apk` running on Android handset or emulator  

---

## Preparation Checklist Before Starting

1. **Device Ready**: Have the Android device unlocked on the Home screen.
2. **Audio / Volume**: Volume set to ~50% (for audio devotional narration and subtle haptics).
3. **Airplane Mode Optional**: Keep Airplane Mode handy to demonstrate true offline capability.
4. **Mindset**: LIFTED is designed to feel like a tranquil, peaceful sanctuary—avoid technical jargon about databases, encryption algorithms, or backends unless Cherie asks.

---

## 1. Opening & Welcome (1 Minute)

> **Presenter**:  
> *"Hi Cherie! It is wonderful to share this moment with you. Today, we are opening the private client preview of **LIFTED v1.0**.*  
>  
> *When we set out to build LIFTED, our guiding heart was to create a safe, faith-based sanctuary for adults and parents healing from trauma and emotional exhaustion. We wanted an app that doesn’t demand daily pressure or streaks, doesn’t sell personal data, and works completely offline whenever someone needs a quiet moment with God.*  
>  
> *Let’s walk through the core experience together."*

---

## 2. The Home Screen & Sanctuary Companion (2 Minutes)

> **Action**: Point to the gentle sky-blue background and the animated Glowing Orb.

> **Presenter**:  
> *"When you open LIFTED, there is no login barrier, no account creation requirement, and no credit card form. You are welcomed immediately into peace.*  
>  
> *Right at the top, the app greets you with a warm reflection: **'Welcome! I'm so glad you're here. How is your day so far?'** You can tap '+ Personalize with your name' at any time.*  
>  
> *Right below is our **Sanctuary Companion**. Notice the feeling pills—Peaceful, Anxious, Grieving, Overwhelmed, Burnout/Tired, Grateful, Lonely, and Holding Steady.*  
>  
> *You can select multiple concurrent feelings because human hearts are complex. For example, a parent can feel both 'Grateful' and 'Burnout/Tired' at the same time."*

> **Action**: Tap **Grateful** and **Burnout / Tired**, then tap **Send** (or type: *"Long week with the kids, but thanking God for small blessings"*).

> **Presenter**:  
> *"Watch how the Companion responds: It doesn't judge, it doesn't give clinical diagnoses, and it doesn't rush you. It offers gentle Scripture, validates your exhaustion, and suggests peaceful next steps like taking a 4-7-8 breath or reading today's Scripture."*

---

## 3. Daily Scripture & Devotionals (Discover Tab) (1.5 Minutes)

> **Action**: Tap the **Discover** tab in the bottom navigation dock.

> **Presenter**:  
> *"Here under Discover, we have our curated faith library. Let’s look at today’s devotional: **'Binding the Broken Heart'** based on Psalm 147:3.*  
>  
> *Notice how it specifically addresses adult and parental healing: recognizing that sudden reactivity or irritation is often an echo of childhood survival habits, and bringing those sore memories into Jesus' gentle hands.*  
>  
> *Each devotional includes a 3-minute reading estimate, a personal reflection prompt, and an audio player for peaceful listening."*

> **Action**: Tap **Play Narration** briefly, then show the topic filters (*Anxiety & Peace*, *Burnout & Rest*, *Parenting Patience*).

---

## 4. 4-7-8 Breathing & Somatic Grounding (1.5 Minutes)

> **Action**: On the Discover screen, scroll to the **4-7-8 Breath Pacer** (under Professional / Somatic Grounding) and tap **Start Breathing**.

> **Presenter**:  
> *"For moments of acute anxiety, panic, or anger, we built an interactive trauma-grounding breath pacer.*  
>  
> *It guides you through proven polyvagal regulation:*  
> - *Inhale for 4 seconds as the circle expands.*  
> - *Hold gently for 7 seconds.*  
> - *Exhale slowly for 8 seconds.*  
>  
> *On physical devices, this is paired with gentle sensory haptics so you can close your eyes and feel the rhythm against your palm without staring at the screen."*

---

## 5. Private Encrypted Journaling & Prayer Requests (Support Tab) (2 Minutes)

> **Action**: Tap the **Support** tab in the bottom dock.

> **Presenter**:  
> *"Under Support, we have two deeply personal spaces: **Private Journal** and **Prayer Requests**.*  
>  
> *In the **Private Journal**, you can write freely or choose guided prompts like 'What is one thing you need to give yourself permission to feel?'. Everything typed here is encrypted locally with hardware-backed security on your phone—not even our servers can read it.*  
>  
> *Notice the draft recovery feature: if you get interrupted by your children or receive a phone call while typing, your unfinished reflection is automatically preserved so your thoughts are never lost."*

> **Action**: Switch sub-tab to **Prayer Requests**.

> **Presenter**:  
> *"Under Prayer Requests, you can lift up personal burdens. Notice our two sample prayers: one community prayer with 31 believers lifting it up, and one private.*  
>  
> *Notice the **3-Day Follow-Up** feature: LIFTED automatically schedules a gentle check-in on the 3rd day to ask how your heart is doing. You can mark whether you are still praying, experiencing peace, or celebrate an answered prayer."*

---

## 6. Sanctuary Privacy Lock & Offline Architecture (1 Minute)

> **Action**: Tap the **My Journey** tab, then tap **Preferences**, and point to **Sanctuary Privacy Lock**.

> **Presenter**:  
> *"Because personal trauma journals and prayers are sacred, LIFTED features a built-in **Sanctuary Privacy Lock**.*  
>  
> *When enabled, whenever you step away from the app, it locks immediately behind your device’s Face ID, Fingerprint, or PIN.*  
>  
> *Even if someone borrows your phone, your private reflections remain completely safe."*

> **Action (Optional)**: Enable Airplane Mode on the handset.

> **Presenter**:  
> *"Notice that even with Airplane Mode turned completely ON, the entire app—companion, prayers, journal, breath pacer, and devotionals—continues working seamlessly. There is zero dependence on remote servers for your daily peace."*

---

## 7. What is Operational vs. Planned for v1.1 (1 Minute)

> **Presenter**:  
> *"To ensure maximum stability for today's preview, here is a quick note on what is operational right now versus what is queued for our upcoming release:*  
>  
> 1. **Operational Today**: Complete offline companion, local encrypted journal, 3-day prayer reminders, mood tracking, devotional reading & audio, breath pacer, and biometric privacy lock.  
> 2. **Planned for v1.1 Launch**: Google Play Billing & Stripe for digital workbooks and donations, live cloud account synchronization across multiple devices, and optional cloud AI companion expansion.*  
>  
> *Everything you saw today runs genuinely on this phone without smoke and mirrors."*
